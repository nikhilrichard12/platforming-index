Supplier change note: second source for cabinet cooling

Norwick Components has quoted an eleven week lead time on the cabinet fan for
Q2, which does not clear the VBS-50 build plan. Purchasing is qualifying a
second source and needs engineering to confirm what else moves with it.

Lines affected by the change:

- Axial fan, 120 mm 24 VDC
- Wire fan guard, 120 mm
- Pan head machine screw, M4 x 10
- Split lock washer, M4
- Nylon cable tie, 150 mm
- Second source qualification plan

The fan sits on six products, so any change here is a change everywhere.
