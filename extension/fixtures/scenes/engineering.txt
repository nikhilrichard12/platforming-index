Half bridge power stage for the VRK-3000 refresh

The refresh keeps today's rating and the same chassis, so the power stage is
the only part of the design that moves. This brief fixes what the
stage is built from before layout starts.

What the stage needs:

- Half bridge IGBT module, 600 V 50 A
- Isolated gate driver board, 6 channel
- DC link capacitor, electrolytic, 470 uF 450 V
- Bleeder resistor, 10k 10 W
- Film capacitor, 20 uF 800 V
- SiC half bridge evaluation module

Open question for review: whether the refresh can hold the existing gate
drive and busbar layout, or whether the SiC study forces a new one.
