Cooling module for the VBS-30 outdoor cabinet

Target is 30 kW continuous in a sealed outdoor enclosure, ambient to 45 C.
The thermal path drives the rest of the design, so this brief fixes it first
and leaves the power stage to the electrical brief.

What the module needs:

- Cooling fan, axial, 120 mm 24 VDC
- Fan guard, wire, 120 mm
- Extruded aluminum heatsink, 200 mm
- Silicone thermal pad, 40 x 40 mm
- Temperature sensor, NTC 10k
- Liquid cooling cold plate
- Variable speed fan controller, 4 pin PWM

Open questions for review: whether the cabinet can take the 120 mm frame
without a new bracket, and whether the cold plate is worth its service burden.
