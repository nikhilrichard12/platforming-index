Custom variant request from Northwind Utilities

Northwind buys roughly 900 inverters a year and wants a variant for their
own field crews. Their standards group sent the list below. They have asked for a
quote in three weeks, so engineering needs to say what is standard and what is
not before we price it.

What the account is asking for:

- LCD display module, 4.3 in
- RS485 Modbus communication card
- Signal 8 pin connector on the front face
- IP65 outdoor enclosure, 10 mm EPDM gasket
- Nylon cable gland, M20
- Branded bezel in the account colour
- Field crew tablet pairing, Bluetooth
