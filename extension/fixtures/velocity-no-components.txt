Market sizing: commercial battery storage, 2026 to 2030

The commercial and industrial storage segment is growing faster than the
residential one, at roughly 14 percent a year through 2030. Demand is
concentrated in three places: data centre backup, light manufacturing, and
retail sites with demand charges worth avoiding.

Pricing has fallen about 9 percent a year since 2022, mostly through cell
chemistry rather than balance of system. Margin therefore sits with whoever
controls installation cost and service.

The main risks are tariff exposure on imported cells, installer availability,
and utility interconnection queues, which now run to eleven months in several
states.
